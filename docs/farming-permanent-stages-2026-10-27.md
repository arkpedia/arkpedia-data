# Permanent alternatives beside the event shop

Reviewed 2026-10-27 using Penguin Statistics Global (`US`) player reports.
Keep each Event Store entry and add a focused permanent stage plus an earlier-stage alternative.
The focused choices minimize sanity per direct target drop among open-ended Global MAIN-stage
samples with at least 1,000 runs. This is not a claim about total side-drop efficiency.
Excluded closed statistical intervals, event stages, unavailable Global stages and small samples.

| Material | Focused stage | Reports | Target drops | Sanity per target | Earlier alternative |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Loxic Kohl | 6-11 | 8319 | 4111 | 42.50 | 2-9 |
| Manganese Ore | 10-16 | 2190 | 1185 | 38.81 | 3-2 |
| Grindstone | 9-16 | 3315 | 1387 | 43.02 | 3-3 |
| RMA70-12 | 9-19 | 4836 | 1954 | 51.97 | 2-10 |

Sanity per target = stage sanity cost × reports ÷ target drops. Newer stages with fewer
reports were not declared better based on noisy samples. These recommendations do not hide
the current chapter shop or add countdowns for distant events.
